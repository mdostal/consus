Logo Concepts

Inline SVG fallback — install the openai-image MCP and re-run /brand-system for image-backed exploration via /logo-exploration.

Every concept below shares one glyph: a vertical kernel of grain with a natural crease down its spine and a small punched "eye" near its base — the seed a granary stores, and the aperture you need counsel to open. The current app icon (a generic hub-and-spoke graph on an indigo field) was an explicit placeholder; these concepts replace the graph-node cliché with something that actually maps to what "Consus" means.

1 · Pure Wordmark

Consus set in Fraunces Bold — the name alone carries the whole personality, for contexts where space is tight or the name itself is the point.

2 · Wordmark + Symbol

The grain-kernel glyph paired with the wordmark, for headers and marketing surfaces where name and mark should register together.

3 · Monogram

An open "C" arc — the granary door, standing ajar — wrapped around the kernel glyph. More identity than a wordmark, less weight than a full badge.

Selected · app icon

4 · Abstract Mark

The kernel alone — its crease and punched "eye" are the whole story. Holds up at 16px, which makes it the candidate for the dock icon, tray icon, and favicon.

5 · Badge / Seal

The kernel ringed by arced "CONSUS," like a Roman coin or wax seal — for a splash screen or About panel where the mythic register can run full ceremonial.
