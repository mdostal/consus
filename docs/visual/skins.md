# Skins

Consus ships four visual skins — genuine per-skin decoration, not just recolored chrome. Switch between them from the masthead or via the `⌘K` command palette.

---

## Granary

The **Granary** skin is the real Consus brand and the default for fresh installs. It applies the full brand system from [`branding/`](https://github.com/mdostal/consus/tree/main/branding): Granary Indigo primary, Threshing Gold accent, Parchment surfaces, and the real Consus brand mark in the masthead.

**When to use:** day-to-day work. It's the brand.

**Palette:**
- Background: Parchment (`#FAF7F0`)
- Primary: Granary Indigo (`#362B6B`)
- Accent: Threshing Gold (`#9C6316`)
- Body text: Quern Charcoal (`#46414F`)

---

## Drafting Table

**Drafting Table** is a light, neutral, technical-document aesthetic — the look of an architect's work surface. Clean whites and greys with precise typographic treatment. For when you want the content to be the focus and the UI to recede.

**When to use:** long reading sessions, detailed API work, anything where visual density and distraction need to be minimal.

---

## Case Board

**Case Board** evokes a legal or investigative surface: deep, considered, formal. Darker than Drafting Table, with a more deliberate pacing. Suited to decision review — when you're reading context carefully and weighing options.

**When to use:** decision queue review, reading long planning docs, anything that benefits from a "slow down and think" visual register.

---

## Harness

**Harness** is a developer-tool aesthetic — dark, terminal-adjacent, with monospace code blocks as first-class citizens. For harness authors and API-first users who live closer to the command line than the planning surface.

**When to use:** API debugging, writing decision payloads, harness development, situations where you want the tool to feel like an extension of the terminal.

---

## Switching skins

**UI:** click the skin selector in the masthead (top right area).

**Command palette:** open `⌘K`, type `skin`, and choose from the list.

The selected skin persists across sessions (stored in the browser).

---

## Skins and themes

Each skin works with Consus's three **themes**: light, dark, and system (follows your OS). The combination of skin × theme gives twelve visual modes — though in practice most people pick one skin and stay in system theme.

See [Themes](themes.md) for how theme switching works.
